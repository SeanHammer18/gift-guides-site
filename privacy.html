Privacy Policy

App: Gift Guides Pin Publisher
Last updated: 2026-10-13

What this app is

Gift Guides Pin Publisher is a private, single-user tool. It publishes pins that the account owner has personally reviewed and approved to the owner's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public and no other Pinterest users can sign in to it.

Data we access

With the owner's permission (via Pinterest OAuth), the app accesses only:

  • The owner's boards (to choose where a pin goes)
  • The owner's pins (to create pins and check they were published)

The app does not request or access advertising, billing, payment or catalog data, and does not access any other user's data.

How data is stored

The access token is stored only in the owner's local macOS Keychain. Pin text and images are stored on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings (Security & logins → Apps), which ends all access. Local copies are deleted on request.
